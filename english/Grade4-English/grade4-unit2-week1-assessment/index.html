How Dorothy Saved the Scarecrow

from The Wonderful Wizard of Oz  ·  by L. Frank Baum

1

While Dorothy was looking earnestly into the odd, painted face of the Scarecrow, she was surprised to see one of the eyes slowly wink at her. She thought she must have been mistaken at first, for none of the scarecrows in Kansas ever wink, but presently the figure nodded its head to her in a friendly way.

2

Then she climbed down from the fence and walked up to it, while Toto ran around the pole and barked.

"Good day," said the Scarecrow, in a rather husky voice.

"Did you speak?" asked the girl, in wonder.

"Certainly," answered the Scarecrow. "How do you do?"

"I'm pretty well, thank you," replied Dorothy politely. "How do you do?"

3

"I'm not feeling well," said the Scarecrow. "It is very tedious being perched up here." The Scarecrow was used to scare away crows.

4

"Can't you get down?" asked Dorothy.

"No, for this pole is stuck up my back. If you will please take away the pole, I shall be greatly obliged to you."

5

Dorothy reached up both arms and lifted the figure off the pole, for, being stuffed with straw, it was quite light.

"Thank you very much," said the Scarecrow, when he had been set down on the ground. "I feel like a new man." Dorothy was puzzled at this, for it sounded odd to hear a stuffed man speak, and to see him bow and walk alongside her.

6

"Who are you?" asked the Scarecrow when he had stretched himself and yawned. "And where are you going?"

"My name is Dorothy," said the girl, "and I am going to Emerald City, to ask the Great Oz to send me back to Kansas."

1In Panel 1, what does the use of the word nodded suggest about the Scarecrow?

2In Panel 2, what does the phrase "in wonder" show about Dorothy's feelings?

3In Panel 5, what do Dorothy's actions of reaching up and lifting the Scarecrow off the pole show about her?

4How does the Scarecrow show that he is curious?

5Write a paragraph that describes what Dorothy's actions reveal about her. Support your answer with details from the text, and include at least two of the words provided in the box below in your answer.

WORD BOX

  • perched
  • puzzled
  • tedious
